# MATH — GRADE 2 (AGES 7–8)

Complete Curriculum Package — All 4 Units
Master Curriculum Project

## 1. Program Overview

| Subject | Math |
|---|---|
| Age Band / Stage | 7–8 (Grade 2) |
| Duration | Full school year — approximately 36 weeks across 4 units |
| Learning Approach | Regrouping, early multiplication, and money/measurement build on Grade 1's place value and time foundations |
| Standards Alignment | US: Common Core 2.OA / 2.NBT / 2.MD  ·  Canada (ON): Grade 2 Math  ·  Global: Cambridge Primary Mathematics Stage 2–3 |
| Assessment Approach | Worksheet → quiz → unit test per unit, plus one cumulative exam guide for parents/teachers |


## 2. Scope & Sequence

Four units cover the full Grade 2 Math year.

### Unit 1: Addition & Subtraction within 100 (Weeks 1–9)

- Add and subtract two-digit numbers within 100, with and without regrouping
- Use place value understanding to add and subtract
- Solve two-step word problems
- Check subtraction using addition
Key vocabulary: regroup, sum, difference, two-step

### Unit 2: Introducing Multiplication (Weeks 10–18)

- Understand multiplication as repeated addition or equal groups
- Skip count by 2s, 5s, and 10s
- Solve simple multiplication problems using arrays or equal groups
- Recognize multiplication as a foundational skill for future division
Key vocabulary: multiply, repeated addition, equal groups, array

### Unit 3: Place Value to 1000 & Money (Weeks 19–27)

- Understand three-digit numbers as hundreds, tens, and ones
- Compare and order three-digit numbers
- Identify coin and bill values and count money combinations
- Make change in simple contexts
Key vocabulary: hundreds, place value, coin, bill, change

### Unit 4: Measurement (Weeks 28–36)

- Measure length using standard units (inches/centimeters)
- Estimate and compare measurements
- Tell time to the nearest 5 minutes
- Read and interpret a simple bar graph
Key vocabulary: measure, estimate, length, bar graph

## 3. Unit 1: Addition & Subtraction within 100


### Lesson Plan — “Adding and Subtracting Two-Digit Numbers”


**Objective**
Students will add and subtract two-digit numbers within 100, using regrouping when needed.

**Materials**
Base-ten blocks, place value mats, whiteboard.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Quick mental math round with easy two-digit plus one-digit problems.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model adding two two-digit numbers with regrouping using base-ten blocks (e.g., 27+15: combine ones, regroup 10 ones into 1 ten).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children practice a few regrouping problems with blocks.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 1 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “When do we need to regroup?”

**Differentiation**
Support: start with problems that don't require regrouping. Stretch: introduce two-step word problems combining addition and subtraction.

## 4. Unit 2: Introducing Multiplication


### Lesson Plan — “Groups and Multiplication”


**Objective**
Students will understand multiplication as repeated addition or equal groups and skip count by 2s, 5s, and 10s.

**Materials**
Counters, egg cartons or small containers for grouping, a hundred chart.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Skip count together by 2s, then 5s, then 10s.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model multiplication as equal groups (3 groups of 4 = 3×4 = 12) using counters, connecting it to repeated addition (4+4+4=12).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children build a few multiplication problems using counters in equal groups.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 2 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “How is multiplication like repeated addition?”

**Differentiation**
Support: keep groups small (2–3 groups of 2–3). Stretch: introduce arrays (rows and columns) as another way to see multiplication.

## 5. Unit 3: Place Value to 1000 & Money


### Lesson Plan — “Hundreds, Tens, Ones, and Money”


**Objective**
Students will understand three-digit place value and count combinations of coins and bills.

**Materials**
Base-ten blocks (including hundreds flats), play coins and bills, place value mats.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Count a group of 100+ objects by grouping into hundreds, tens, and ones.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model building three-digit numbers with base-ten blocks (234 = 2 hundreds + 3 tens + 4 ones). Introduce coin values (penny, nickel, dime, quarter) and practice counting simple combinations.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children build numbers with blocks and count small coin combinations together.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 3 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Show a small coin combination; children count the total together.

**Differentiation**
Support: keep numbers under 300 and coin combinations to 2–3 coins. Stretch: introduce making change from a dollar for simple purchases.

## 6. Unit 4: Measurement


### Lesson Plan — “Measuring Our World”


**Objective**
Students will measure length using standard units, tell time to the nearest 5 minutes, and read a simple bar graph.

**Materials**
Rulers, a large clock with movable hands, a simple bar graph template.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Estimate the length of a pencil, then measure it with a ruler to check.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Practice measuring several classroom objects with a ruler in inches or centimeters, recording results. Model reading a clock to the nearest 5 minutes.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children measure objects and set clock times called out by the teacher.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 4 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Show a simple bar graph; ask a question about it.

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on whole-inch measurements and o'clock/half-hour times. Stretch: introduce measuring to the nearest half-inch and telling time to the minute.

## 7. Cumulative Exam & Assessment Guide (For Parents & Teachers)

This guide covers the full Grade 2 Math year (all 4 units).

**End-of-Year Skills Checklist**
- Adds and subtracts within 100 with regrouping
- Understands multiplication as repeated addition/equal groups and skip counts fluently
- Understands three-digit place value and counts money combinations
- Measures length, tells time to 5 minutes, and reads bar graphs

**Question Types to Expect**
Computation with regrouping, skip counting, multiplication word problems, place value and money counting, and measurement or graph reading.

**Tips for Supporting Your Child**
- Practice regrouping with real objects (beans, blocks) before moving to paper
- Count change together during errands or pretend play
- Measure things around the house together
- Read bar graphs in books or the news together

**Suggested Pacing (Full Year)**
Each unit follows: Worksheet → 2–3 days of practice → Quiz → about 1 week more practice → Unit Test → move to the next unit, across roughly 36 weeks.
