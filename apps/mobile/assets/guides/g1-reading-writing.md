# READING & WRITING — GRADE 1 (AGES 6–7)

Complete Curriculum Package — All 4 Units
Master Curriculum Project

## 1. Program Overview

| Subject | Reading & Writing |
|---|---|
| Age Band / Stage | 6–7 (Grade 1) |
| Duration | Full school year — approximately 36 weeks across 4 units |
| Learning Approach | Builds directly on Kindergarten phonics; daily independent reading time grows steadily alongside more structured writing practice |

## 2. Scope & Sequence

Four units cover the full Grade 1 Reading & Writing year, building on Kindergarten letter-sound and CVC-word foundations.

### Unit 1: Digraphs & Blends (Weeks 1–9)

- Read words with common consonant digraphs (sh, ch, th, wh)
- Read words with consonant blends (bl, st, tr, and similar)
- Segment and blend words containing digraphs and blends
- Spell simple digraph and blend words
Key vocabulary: digraph, blend, consonant, sound

### Unit 2: Fluency & Sight Words (Weeks 10–18)

- Read an expanded list of grade 1 high-frequency sight words (they, was, said, have, from, were, what, when)
- Read short decodable passages with increasing fluency
- Self-correct when a word doesn't make sense
- Build reading stamina with short independent reading time
Key vocabulary: fluency, sight word, passage

### Unit 3: Sentences & Punctuation (Weeks 19–27)

- Write complete sentences with correct capitalization and end punctuation
- Use question marks and exclamation points appropriately
- Combine simple sentences using ‘and’
- Begin using descriptive words (adjectives) in writing
Key vocabulary: sentence, question mark, exclamation point, adjective

### Unit 4: Reading for Meaning (Weeks 28–36)

- Identify the main idea and key details in a short text
- Distinguish between fiction and nonfiction
- Answer comprehension questions (who, what, where, when, why)
- Make simple predictions and connections while reading
Key vocabulary: main idea, detail, fiction, nonfiction, predict

## 3. Unit 1: Digraphs & Blends


### Lesson Plan — “Digraph Detectives”


**Objective**
Students will read and spell simple words containing common consonant digraphs (sh, ch, th, wh).

**Materials**
Digraph picture and word cards, whiteboard, digraph sorting mats.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Say a digraph sound (/sh/); children think of a word that starts with that sound.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce each digraph with a key picture (ship=sh, chair=ch, thumb=th, whale=wh). Model reading a few words with each digraph.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children sort word cards by digraph sound.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 1 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children think of one new word with a digraph and share it.

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on one digraph at a time with strong picture support. Stretch: introduce blends (bl, st, tr) alongside digraphs for comparison.

## 4. Unit 2: Fluency & Sight Words


### Lesson Plan — “Sight Word Fluency Practice”


**Objective**
Students will read an expanded set of grade 1 sight words and short decodable passages with increasing fluency.

**Materials**
Sight word flashcards (they, was, said, have, from, were, what, when), short decodable passage sheets.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Quick sight word flashcard round.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Read a short decodable passage together as a class, pointing to each word.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children take turns reading the passage aloud to each other, practicing smooth phrasing.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 2 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask a comprehension question about the passage read together.

**Differentiation**
Support: shorten the passage and pre-teach tricky words. Stretch: introduce a slightly longer passage with more sight words.

## 5. Unit 3: Sentences & Punctuation


### Lesson Plan — “Building Better Sentences”


**Objective**
Students will write complete sentences with correct capitalization, end punctuation, and simple descriptive words.

**Materials**
Sentence strips, whiteboard, picture cards for descriptive writing prompts.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Show a sentence missing capitalization and punctuation; children fix it together.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model writing a sentence about a picture, including a describing word: “The fluffy dog runs.” Discuss the capital letter, describing word, and period.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
As a class, build 2–3 sentences together using picture prompts and describing words.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 3 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children read their favorite sentence from the worksheet aloud.

**Differentiation**
Support: provide a sentence frame with a blank for the describing word. Stretch: ask children to combine two short sentences into one using ‘and.’

### Practice Worksheet

5. Add a describing word: “The ___ cat slept.”
6. Combine using ‘and’: “I like dogs. I like cats.”
7. Write a question about your favorite animal.
8. Write an exclamation about something exciting.
9. Write a complete sentence with a describing word about a picture in your head.

## 6. Unit 4: Reading for Meaning


### Lesson Plan — “What's It Really About?”


**Objective**
Students will identify the main idea and key details of a short text and distinguish fiction from nonfiction.

**Materials**
A short fiction book and a short nonfiction book on a similar topic, chart paper.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What's the difference between a made-up story and a true book?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Read a short passage aloud. As a class, identify what it's “mostly about” (main idea) and 2–3 supporting details.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
Compare the fiction and nonfiction books side by side, sorting features into two columns.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 4 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask children to name one fiction and one nonfiction book they've read.

**Differentiation**
Support: use very short, clearly contrasted passages. Stretch: ask children to write their own one-sentence main-idea summary of a longer text.

## 7. Cumulative Exam & Assessment Guide (For Parents & Teachers)

This guide covers the full Grade 1 Reading & Writing year (all 4 units).

**End-of-Year Skills Checklist**
- Reads and spells words with common digraphs and blends
- Reads an expanded set of sight words with growing fluency
- Writes complete sentences with correct capitalization, punctuation, and describing words
- Identifies the main idea and details, and distinguishes fiction from nonfiction

**Question Types to Expect**
Digraph and blend identification, sight word recognition, sentence correction and writing, main idea and detail questions, and fiction vs. nonfiction sorting.

**Tips for Supporting Your Child**
- Read together daily, pausing to ask ‘what's this mostly about?’
- Praise attempts at writing complete sentences even with spelling errors
- Point out real vs. make-believe in everyday books and shows
- Celebrate growing reading stamina, not just speed

**Suggested Pacing (Full Year)**
Each unit follows: Worksheet → 2–3 days of practice → Quiz → about 1 week more practice → Unit Test → move to the next unit, across roughly 36 weeks.
