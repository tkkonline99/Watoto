| Subject | Science |

## 2. Scope & Sequence

Four units cover the full Grade 1 Science year.

### Unit 1: Plant & Animal Life Cycles (Weeks 1–9)

- Describe the life cycle of a plant (seed, sprout, plant, flower, seed)
- Describe the life cycle of an animal (e.g., butterfly or frog)
- Compare life cycles of different living things
- Identify what living things need to grow and survive
Key vocabulary: life cycle, seed, sprout, larva, tadpole, adult

### Unit 2: Earth's Materials (Weeks 10–18)

- Identify common earth materials (rock, soil, sand, water)
- Describe basic properties of rocks and soil
- Sort materials by properties (hard/soft, rough/smooth)
- Understand that earth materials can be used for different purposes
Key vocabulary: rock, soil, sand, mineral, property

### Unit 3: Seasons & Weather Patterns (Weeks 19–27)

- Describe patterns in weather across the four seasons
- Record and graph simple weather data over time
- Explain how seasonal weather patterns affect plants, animals, and people
- Predict weather based on observed patterns
Key vocabulary: pattern, temperature, precipitation, forecast

### Unit 4: Simple Forces & Sound (Weeks 28–36)

- Investigate how pushes and pulls of different strengths affect motion
- Understand that sound is caused by vibration
- Explore how objects can be made to make sound
- Conduct and record a simple force or sound investigation
Key vocabulary: force, vibration, pitch, volume

## 3. Unit 1: Plant & Animal Life Cycles


### Lesson Plan — “From Seed to Plant, Egg to Butterfly”


**Objective**
Students will describe and sequence the life cycle of a plant and an animal.

**Materials**
Life cycle picture cards (plant and butterfly or frog), seeds, a small pot of soil (optional planting activity).

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What do you think happens after a seed is planted?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Show and sequence plant life cycle cards (seed → sprout → plant → flower → seed). Do the same for an animal life cycle (egg → larva/tadpole → pupa/tadpole with legs → adult).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children mix up and re-sequence a set of life cycle cards.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 1 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask children to name one thing that stays the same and one thing that changes across a life cycle.

**Differentiation**
Support: use just 3–4 stage picture cards with strong visual support. Stretch: compare the plant life cycle to the animal life cycle, noting similarities and differences.

## 4. Unit 2: Earth's Materials


### Lesson Plan — “Exploring Earth's Materials”


**Objective**
Students will identify and describe properties of common earth materials like rock, soil, and sand.

**Materials**
Samples of rocks, soil, and sand (or pictures), magnifying glasses (optional), sorting mats.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What is under our feet outside? What is it made of?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Examine rock, soil, and sand samples. Describe properties (hard/soft, rough/smooth, big/small pieces).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children sort a mixed set of materials by property (texture, size).

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 2 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What is one way people use rocks or soil?”

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on just 2 materials (rock vs. soil) with hands-on samples. Stretch: introduce the idea that rocks can be broken down into smaller pieces (sand, soil) over time.

## 5. Unit 3: Seasons & Weather Patterns


### Lesson Plan — “Tracking Weather Patterns”


**Objective**
Students will record simple weather data over time and describe patterns across seasons.

**Materials**
A weather chart/calendar, weather symbol stickers or drawings, a simple bar graph template.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What was the weather like yesterday? Is it the same today?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Record the day's weather on a class chart. Look back at previous entries and discuss any patterns noticed.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
As a class, turn a week of weather data into a simple bar graph, discussing which type of weather happened most.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 3 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “Based on our graph, what weather do you predict for tomorrow? Why?”

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on just noticing ‘same or different’ day to day. Stretch: have children make and explain a weather prediction based on a full month of data.

## 6. Unit 4: Simple Forces & Sound


### Lesson Plan — “Forces and Sounds All Around”


**Objective**
Students will investigate how pushes and pulls affect motion and explore how vibration creates sound.

**Materials**
Balls of different sizes, a rubber band, a simple drum or container to tap, a ramp.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Pluck a rubber band and ask children what they notice.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Roll balls down a ramp with different amounts of push, observing how a bigger push makes the ball go farther/faster. Explore sound by tapping objects and feeling vibrations.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children experiment with pushing objects with different strength and describe what happens; then experiment making different sounds.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 4 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What did you notice about how sound is made?”

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on push/pull only, or sound only, depending on time. Stretch: have children design their own simple investigation testing how push strength affects distance.

## 7. Cumulative Exam & Assessment Guide (For Parents & Teachers)

This guide covers the full Grade 1 Science year (all 4 units).

**End-of-Year Skills Checklist**
- Sequences plant and animal life cycles
- Identifies earth materials and their properties
- Records and interprets simple weather data and patterns
- Understands that pushes/pulls affect motion and sound comes from vibration

**Question Types to Expect**
Sequencing, matching and circling, graph reading, true/false, and simple investigations with explanations.

**Tips for Supporting Your Child**
- Grow something together at home, even a windowsill herb
- Collect and examine rocks and soil on walks
- Keep a simple family weather log for a week
- Experiment with sound using kitchen items (pots, rubber bands, containers)

**Suggested Pacing (Full Year)**
Each unit follows: Worksheet → 2–3 days of practice → Quiz → about 1 week more practice → Unit Test → move to the next unit, across roughly 36 weeks.
