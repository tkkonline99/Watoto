# READING & WRITING — GRADE 3 (AGES 8–9)

Complete Curriculum Package — All 4 Units
Master Curriculum Project

## 1. Program Overview

| Subject | Reading & Writing |
|---|---|
| Age Band / Stage | 8–9 (Grade 3) |
| Duration | Full school year — approximately 36 weeks across 4 units |
| Learning Approach | Multi-paragraph writing, research skills, and figurative language deepen; reading spans a wider range of genres |
| Standards Alignment | US: Common Core RL.3 / RI.3 / W.3 / L.3  ·  Canada (ON): Grade 3 Language  ·  Global: Cambridge Primary English Stage 3–4 |
| Assessment Approach | Worksheet → quiz → unit test per unit, plus one cumulative exam guide for parents/teachers |


## 2. Scope & Sequence

Four units cover the full Grade 3 Reading & Writing year.

### Unit 1: Multi-Paragraph Writing (Weeks 1–9)

- Write a multi-paragraph piece with an introduction, body, and conclusion
- Group related information into paragraphs
- Use linking words and phrases to connect ideas
- Plan writing using a simple graphic organizer
Key vocabulary: introduction, body, conclusion, paragraph, linking word

### Unit 2: Parts of Speech (Weeks 10–18)

- Identify and use nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs
- Use correct verb tenses (past, present, future)
- Understand subject-verb and pronoun-antecedent agreement
- Identify and correct common grammar errors
Key vocabulary: pronoun, adverb, verb tense, antecedent

### Unit 3: Figurative Language (Weeks 19–27)

- Identify and interpret similes and metaphors
- Recognize idioms and their meanings
- Understand personification in simple texts
- Use figurative language in their own writing
Key vocabulary: simile, metaphor, idiom, personification

### Unit 4: Research Skills & Genres (Weeks 28–36)

- Gather information from multiple sources on a topic
- Distinguish and read across varied genres (poetry, fables, informational text)
- Take simple notes and organize information
- Write a short informational report
Key vocabulary: source, research, genre, notes, report

## 3. Unit 1: Multi-Paragraph Writing


### Lesson Plan — “Introduction, Body, Conclusion”


**Objective**
Students will plan and write a multi-paragraph piece with an introduction, body, and conclusion.

**Materials**
Graphic organizer handout (intro/body/conclusion boxes), sample multi-paragraph text, lined paper.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Show a strong intro sentence and a weak one; discuss which makes you want to read more.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model planning a 3-paragraph piece using a graphic organizer: introduction (topic + hook), body (details), conclusion (wrap-up). Discuss linking words (also, in addition, finally).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
As a class, fill in a graphic organizer for a shared topic.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 1 practice worksheet (plan and begin a multi-paragraph piece).

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children share their introduction paragraph with a partner.

**Differentiation**
Support: provide sentence starters for each paragraph. Stretch: add a second body paragraph with its own supporting details.

## 4. Unit 2: Parts of Speech


### Lesson Plan — “Parts of Speech in Action”


**Objective**
Students will identify and use nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs, with correct verb tenses.

**Materials**
Parts-of-speech reference chart, word sort cards, sentence-building materials.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Review nouns, verbs, and adjectives from Grade 2; ask for an example of each.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce pronouns (he, she, it, they) and adverbs (words that describe verbs, often ending in -ly). Model a sentence labeling all parts of speech. Review verb tenses (walked/walk/will walk).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children sort word cards into parts-of-speech categories.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 2 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children build one sentence using at least a noun, verb, adjective, and adverb.

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on pronouns and adverbs, reviewing prior parts. Stretch: introduce pronoun-antecedent agreement and irregular verbs.

## 5. Unit 3: Figurative Language


### Lesson Plan — “Painting Pictures with Words”


**Objective**
Students will identify and interpret similes, metaphors, idioms, and personification.

**Materials**
Figurative language example cards, a chart with the four types, picture prompts.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Say 'She was as busy as a bee.' Ask what that really means.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce each type: simile (compares using like/as), metaphor (says something IS something else), idiom (a phrase that means something different from the literal words), personification (giving human traits to non-human things). Give examples of each.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children sort example cards into the four types.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 3 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children write one simile of their own and share it.

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on similes and idioms first. Stretch: have children write an example of each type.

## 6. Unit 4: Research Skills & Genres


### Lesson Plan — “Becoming a Researcher”


**Objective**
Students will gather information from multiple sources, take simple notes, and write a short informational report.

**Materials**
Two simple sources on one topic (books or printouts), a note-taking template, lined paper.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “If you wanted to learn all about penguins, where could you find information?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model taking notes from two sources on one topic, writing key facts in your own words. Discuss organizing notes into categories, then turning them into a short report.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
As a class, take a few notes from a shared source and group them.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 4 practice worksheet (take notes and begin a mini-report).

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children share one interesting fact they found.

**Differentiation**
Support: provide a note template with categories filled in. Stretch: require using two sources and citing where each fact came from.

## 7. Cumulative Exam & Assessment Guide (For Parents & Teachers)

This guide covers the full Grade 3 Reading & Writing year (all 4 units).

**End-of-Year Skills Checklist**
- Writes a multi-paragraph piece with introduction, body, and conclusion
- Identifies and uses all major parts of speech with correct verb tenses
- Identifies and interprets similes, metaphors, idioms, and personification
- Gathers information from multiple sources and writes a short informational report

**Question Types to Expect**
Multi-paragraph writing, parts-of-speech identification and correction, figurative language interpretation, and research/note-taking tasks.

**Tips for Supporting Your Child**
- Encourage longer writing with clear beginnings, middles, and ends
- Play 'spot the figurative language' while reading together
- Look up interesting topics together using multiple sources
- Talk about different genres and what makes each unique

**Suggested Pacing (Full Year)**
Each unit follows: Worksheet → 2–3 days of practice → Quiz → about 1 week more practice → Unit Test → move to the next unit, across roughly 36 weeks.
