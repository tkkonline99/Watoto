## 1. Program Overview

| Subject | Math |
|---|---|
| Age Band / Stage | 9–10 (Grade 4) |
| Duration | Full school year — approximately 36 weeks across 4 units |
| Learning Approach | Multi-digit multiplication/division, fraction operations, and decimals build toward the abstraction of upper elementary math |

## 2. Scope & Sequence

Four units cover the full Grade 4 Math year, the final band in this curriculum.

### Unit 1: Multi-Digit Multiplication & Division (Weeks 1–9)

- Multiply a multi-digit number by a one-digit number
- Multiply two two-digit numbers
- Divide multi-digit numbers by a one-digit divisor, with remainders
- Solve multi-step word problems using all four operations
Key vocabulary: product, quotient, remainder, factor, dividend

### Unit 2: Fraction Equivalence & Operations (Weeks 10–18)

- Recognize and generate equivalent fractions
- Compare fractions with different numerators and denominators
- Add and subtract fractions with like denominators
- Understand a fraction as a multiple of a unit fraction
Key vocabulary: equivalent, numerator, denominator, unit fraction, mixed number

### Unit 3: Decimals & Place Value (Weeks 19–27)

- Understand decimal notation for fractions (tenths, hundredths)
- Compare decimals to the hundredths place
- Relate fractions and decimals (1/2 = 0.5)
- Understand place value in numbers to the millions
Key vocabulary: decimal, tenths, hundredths, place value

### Unit 4: Angles, Geometry & Factors (Weeks 28–36)

- Identify and measure angles (right, acute, obtuse)
- Classify shapes by properties (lines, angles, symmetry)
- Find factors and multiples of numbers
- Identify prime and composite numbers
Key vocabulary: angle, acute, obtuse, factor, multiple, prime, composite

## 3. Unit 1: Multi-Digit Multiplication & Division


### Lesson Plan — “Multiplying and Dividing Bigger Numbers”


**Objective**
Students will multiply multi-digit numbers and divide with remainders.

**Materials**
Grid/area model paper, place value chart, whiteboard.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Review a few multiplication facts and one simple division.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model multiplying a 2-digit by 1-digit number using the area model and standard algorithm. Model dividing a 2-3 digit number by a 1-digit divisor, including interpreting a remainder.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children solve a few multiplication and division problems.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 1 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What does the remainder mean in a division problem?”

**Differentiation**
Support: use the area model with grid paper. Stretch: multiply two 2-digit numbers and solve multi-step problems.

## 4. Unit 2: Fraction Equivalence & Operations


### Lesson Plan — “Working with Fractions”


**Objective**
Students will generate equivalent fractions and add and subtract fractions with like denominators.

**Materials**
Fraction strips, number lines, fraction circles.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Show 1/2 and 2/4 with fraction strips; ask if they're equal.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model generating equivalent fractions by multiplying numerator and denominator by the same number. Model adding and subtracting fractions with like denominators (keep the denominator, add/subtract numerators).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children generate equivalents and solve a few like-denominator problems.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 2 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “Why do you keep the denominator the same when adding fractions with the same bottom number?”

**Differentiation**
Support: use fraction strips for visual support. Stretch: introduce mixed numbers and simple unlike denominators.

## 5. Unit 3: Decimals & Place Value


### Lesson Plan — “Decimals and Big Numbers”


**Objective**
Students will understand decimal notation, compare decimals, and relate fractions to decimals.

**Materials**
Place value charts (including decimal places), grid models for tenths/hundredths, number lines.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Show $0.50 and 1/2; ask how they're related.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce decimals as another way to show fractions of tenths and hundredths (0.5 = 5/10 = 1/2). Model comparing decimals using place value. Review place value up to the millions.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children write fractions as decimals and compare decimal pairs.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 3 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “How are fractions and decimals related?”

**Differentiation**
Support: use money (dollars/cents) as a decimal model. Stretch: compare and order several decimals and relate them to fractions.

## 6. Unit 4: Angles, Geometry & Factors


### Lesson Plan — “Angles, Shapes, and Factors”


**Objective**
Students will identify angles, classify shapes, and find factors, multiples, and prime numbers.

**Materials**
Protractors (optional), shape cards, grid paper, a hundred chart.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Show a right angle (corner of paper); ask what other angles look like.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce angle types: right (90°), acute (smaller), obtuse (larger). Classify shapes by their angles and lines. Then find factors (numbers that divide evenly) and multiples, and introduce prime (only 2 factors) vs. composite numbers.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children classify angles/shapes and list factors of a number.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 4 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What makes a number prime?”

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on angle types and simple factors. Stretch: identify prime numbers up to 50 and find common factors.

## 7. Cumulative Exam & Assessment Guide (For Parents & Teachers)

This guide covers the full Grade 4 Math year (all 4 units) — the final band in the curriculum.

**End-of-Year Skills Checklist**
- Multiplies multi-digit numbers and divides with remainders
- Generates equivalent fractions and adds/subtracts fractions with like denominators
- Understands decimals, compares them, and relates them to fractions
- Identifies angles, classifies shapes, and finds factors, multiples, and primes

**Question Types to Expect**
Multi-digit computation and word problems, fraction equivalence and operations, decimal notation and comparison, and geometry/factor questions.

**Tips for Supporting Your Child**
- Practice multi-digit multiplication with real-world quantities
- Cook together to explore fractions and equivalents
- Use money to connect decimals and fractions
- Spot angles and shapes in the environment, and play factor games

**Suggested Pacing (Full Year)**
Each unit follows: Worksheet → 2–3 days of practice → Quiz → about 1 week more practice → Unit Test → move to the next unit, across roughly 36 weeks.
