## 1. Program Overview

| Subject | Math |
|---|---|
| Age Band / Stage | 6–7 (Grade 1) |
| Duration | Full school year — approximately 36 weeks across 4 units |
| Learning Approach | Strategy-based fact fluency (counting on, making ten) plus hands-on place value, time, and fraction work |
| Standards Alignment | US: Common Core 1.OA / 1.NBT / 1.MD  ·  Canada (ON): Grade 1 Math  ·  Global: Cambridge Primary Mathematics Stage 1–2 |

## 2. Scope & Sequence

Four units cover the full Grade 1 Math year, building on Kindergarten counting and number-bond foundations.

### Unit 1: Addition & Subtraction within 20 (Weeks 1–9)

- Add and subtract fluently within 20
- Use strategies like counting on, making ten, and doubles
- Solve simple word problems involving addition and subtraction within 20
- Understand the relationship between addition and subtraction (fact families)
Key vocabulary: sum, difference, fact family, doubles, counting on

### Unit 2: Place Value — Tens & Ones (Weeks 10–18)

- Understand two-digit numbers as tens and ones
- Compare two-digit numbers using greater than / less than / equal
- Add a two-digit number and a one-digit number
- Count to 120 starting at any number
Key vocabulary: tens, ones, place value, greater than, less than

### Unit 3: Telling Time (Weeks 19–27)

- Tell time to the hour and half hour on an analog clock
- Understand the difference between the hour hand and minute hand
- Relate time to daily routines
- Write time in digital format matching an analog clock
Key vocabulary: hour, half hour, clock, hour hand, minute hand

### Unit 4: Introducing Fractions (Weeks 28–36)

- Understand halves and fourths as equal parts of a whole
- Divide shapes into two or four equal parts
- Recognize that more equal parts means smaller pieces
- Identify halves and fourths in everyday contexts
Key vocabulary: half, fourth/quarter, equal parts, whole

## 3. Unit 1: Addition & Subtraction within 20


### Lesson Plan — “Strategies for Adding and Subtracting”


**Objective**
Students will add and subtract fluently within 20 using strategies like counting on and making ten.

**Materials**
Counters or blocks, ten-frames, a number line, whiteboard.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Quick doubles facts round (2+2, 3+3, 5+5) using fingers.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model ‘counting on’ for addition (8+3: start at 8, count on 3 more) and ‘making ten’ (9+4: take 1 from 4 to make 9 into 10, then add 3 more = 13) using a ten-frame.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children practice a few problems using counters and ten-frames, checking each other's strategies.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 1 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What strategy did you use to solve 9+5?” Discuss a few different valid approaches.

**Differentiation**
Support: keep sums within 10 using physical counters. Stretch: introduce related subtraction facts (fact families) alongside each addition fact.

## 4. Unit 2: Place Value — Tens & Ones


### Lesson Plan — “Tens and Ones”


**Objective**
Students will understand two-digit numbers as a combination of tens and ones, and compare two-digit numbers.

**Materials**
Base-ten blocks (or bundles of 10 straws plus single straws), place value mats, number cards.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Count a pile of 24 objects by grouping into tens and counting leftovers.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model building numbers with base-ten blocks (e.g., 34 = 3 tens + 4 ones), writing the number on a place value mat.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children build 3–4 numbers using base-ten blocks and record them on place value mats.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 2 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Show two numbers built with blocks; children compare which is greater.

**Differentiation**
Support: keep numbers under 30 with concrete blocks. Stretch: introduce comparing numbers up to 100 using <, >, = symbols.

## 5. Unit 3: Telling Time


### Lesson Plan — “What Time Is It?”


**Objective**
Students will tell time to the hour and half hour on an analog clock.

**Materials**
A large demonstration clock with movable hands, individual student clocks, daily schedule pictures.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What time do you wake up? What time do you eat dinner?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce the hour hand (short) and minute hand (long). Model setting the clock to o'clock times (3:00, 7:00) and half-hour times (3:30, 7:30).

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children take turns setting their own clocks to times called out by the teacher.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 3 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Show a clock; children write the matching digital time.

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on o'clock times only first. Stretch: introduce quarter-hour times (:15, :45) for extra challenge.

## 6. Unit 4: Introducing Fractions


### Lesson Plan — “Sharing Equally: Halves and Fourths”


**Objective**
Students will understand halves and fourths as equal parts of a whole and divide shapes accordingly.

**Materials**
Paper circles and squares, scissors, crayons, real objects to ‘share.’

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “If we have one cookie and two friends, how could we share it fairly?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Fold a paper circle in half, showing two equal parts. Fold again to show fourths. Discuss that ‘equal’ means the same size.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children fold and cut their own shapes into halves and fourths, checking that the parts are equal.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 4 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask: “Which is bigger, a half or a fourth of the same pizza? Why?”

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on halves only with very simple shapes. Stretch: introduce the idea that more equal parts means smaller pieces, comparing halves vs. fourths vs. eighths conceptually.

## 7. Cumulative Exam & Assessment Guide (For Parents & Teachers)

This guide covers the full Grade 1 Math year (all 4 units).

**End-of-Year Skills Checklist**
- Adds and subtracts fluently within 20 using strategies
- Understands tens and ones place value and compares two-digit numbers
- Tells time to the hour and half hour
- Understands halves and fourths as equal parts of a whole

**Question Types to Expect**
Fact fluency, place value identification, time-telling, fraction identification and shading, word problems, and short explanations.

**Tips for Supporting Your Child**
- Practice math facts in short, frequent bursts rather than long sessions
- Use real coins, clocks, and food at home for hands-on practice
- Celebrate strategy explanations, not just correct answers
- Point out time and fractions in everyday life (cooking, schedules)

**Suggested Pacing (Full Year)**
Each unit follows: Worksheet → 2–3 days of practice → Quiz → about 1 week more practice → Unit Test → move to the next unit, across roughly 36 weeks.
