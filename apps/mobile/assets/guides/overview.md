# Master Curriculum Overview

Master Curriculum Scope & Sequence — Ages 0–10

> Blended Canada (Ontario as reference province) / US (Common Core, NGSS, C3, CSTA) / Global (Cambridge Primary, IB PYP) approach — see ‘Notes & Assumptions’ tab


## Ages 0–1 · Babies & Toddlers

- **Reading & Writing** — Language exposure & bonding — read-alouds, rhymes, sound play, book handling *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Math** — Object exploration, size/shape awareness, simple more/less through play *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Science** — Sensory exploration, cause & effect, observing surroundings *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Social Studies** — Recognizing family faces & voices, emerging sense of self *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Technology & Logic** — Cause-and-effect toys, simple problem-solving through play *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Storybooks** — Board books, high-contrast images, simple rhymes *Assessment: Reading-level tracking only*

## Ages 2–3 · Babies & Toddlers

- **Reading & Writing** — Vocabulary growth, picture naming, phrase repetition, pretend reading, page turning *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Math** — Rote counting 1–10, shape & color sorting, simple patterns *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Science** — Naming animals/objects, exploring textures & materials, simple weather awareness *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Social Studies** — Family members, familiar routines, basic emotions *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Technology & Logic** — Sorting & matching, simple puzzles, pattern recognition *Assessment: Milestone checklist only (no formal tests)*
- **Storybooks** — Picture books, repetitive/rhyming text, lift-the-flap books *Assessment: Reading-level tracking only*

## Ages 3–4 · Preschool

- **Reading & Writing** — Rhyming & syllable awareness, letter recognition (name + a few others), name-writing attempt, listening comprehension *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Math** — Counting to 10–20, number recognition, one-to-one correspondence, basic shapes *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Science** — Five senses, living vs. non-living, plant & animal basics *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Social Studies** — Community helpers, family structures, self vs. others *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Technology & Logic** — Sequencing (first/next/last), simple patterns, shape puzzles *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Storybooks** — Picture books with simple plots, read-aloud favorites *Assessment: Reading-level tracking only*

## Ages 4–5 · Preschool

- **Reading & Writing** — Letter-sound correspondence (most letters), blending/segmenting basics, emergent writing (labels, letter strings) *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Math** — Counting to 20+, number writing, addition/subtraction with objects, comparing quantities *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Science** — Life cycles intro, seasons, needs of living things, simple experiments (sink/float) *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Social Studies** — Neighborhood & community, rules & cooperation, cultural celebrations intro *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Technology & Logic** — Multi-step directions, logic puzzles, unplugged ‘coding’ sequencing games, digital-citizenship intro *Assessment: Observation checklist; light informal activities*
- **Storybooks** — Longer picture-book narratives, alphabet & counting books *Assessment: Reading-level tracking only*

## Ages 5–6 · Kindergarten

- **Reading & Writing** — Full alphabet + sounds, CVC word decoding, core sight words, simple sentence writing, story retelling *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Math** — Counting to 100, addition/subtraction within 10, number bonds, shapes & measurement, simple graphs *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Science** — Living things & habitats, weather & seasons, five senses, push/pull forces *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Social Studies** — Self, family & community, rules & fairness, classroom/school mapping, cultural diversity intro *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Technology & Logic** — Unplugged coding concepts (sequences/loops via games), pattern logic, keyboard/mouse basics, digital safety intro *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Storybooks** — Early readers with predictable text, simple decodable books *Assessment: Reading-level tracking + light comprehension checks*

## Ages 6–7 · Grade 1

- **Reading & Writing** — Fluent CVC + digraph decoding, expanded sight words, sentence-level writing, basic punctuation, main-idea comprehension *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Math** — Addition/subtraction within 20, place value (tens/ones), telling time, basic fractions (halves) *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Science** — Plant/animal life cycles, earth materials, seasons & weather patterns, simple forces *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Social Studies** — Community roles & jobs, needs vs. wants, basic maps & globes, past vs. present *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Technology & Logic** — Beginner block-based coding, logical-reasoning puzzles, typing basics, internet safety *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Storybooks** — Early chapter books, levelled readers *Assessment: Reading-level tracking + light comprehension checks*

## Ages 7–8 · Grade 2

- **Reading & Writing** — Reading fluency growth, paragraph writing, spelling patterns, inferential comprehension, grammar basics *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Math** — Addition/subtraction within 100, intro multiplication, place value to 1000, money, measurement *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Science** — Habitats & adaptation, states of matter, landforms, simple engineering challenges *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Social Studies** — Local history, continents & oceans, citizenship & rules, cultures around the world *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Technology & Logic** — Block-based coding (simple programs), debugging intro, basic search literacy, safe online habits *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Storybooks** — Chapter books, series books, growing independence *Assessment: Reading-level tracking + light comprehension checks*

## Ages 8–9 · Grade 3

- **Reading & Writing** — Multi-paragraph writing, intro research skills, figurative language, parts of speech, varied genres *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Math** — Multiplication/division facts, fractions as numbers, area & perimeter, multi-digit addition/subtraction *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Science** — Life cycles & ecosystems, forces & motion, weather vs. climate, day/night & moon phases *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Social Studies** — Communities & government basics, regions & geography, local history, goods & services *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Technology & Logic** — Block-based coding (loops & conditionals), computational thinking, how computers work (intro) *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Storybooks** — Longer chapter books, varied genres (fantasy, realistic fiction) *Assessment: Reading-level tracking + light comprehension checks*

## Ages 9–10 · Grade 4

- **Reading & Writing** — Essay structure (narrative/persuasive/informational), vocabulary in context, close reading, grammar mastery *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Math** — Multi-digit multiplication/division, fraction equivalence & operations, decimals intro, angles, factors/multiples *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Science** — Energy & simple machines, food webs, erosion & rock-cycle intro, solar system basics *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Social Studies** — Provincial/state history, government structures, regions & resources, intro to trade *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Technology & Logic** — Advanced block coding / beginner text-code intro, algorithms & problem-solving, digital citizenship (privacy) *Assessment: Full: worksheets, quiz, unit test, exam guide*
- **Storybooks** — Middle-grade novels, nonfiction trade books, genre exploration *Assessment: Reading-level tracking + light comprehension checks*
