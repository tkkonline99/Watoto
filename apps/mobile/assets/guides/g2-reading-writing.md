# READING & WRITING — GRADE 2 (AGES 7–8)

Complete Curriculum Package — All 4 Units
Master Curriculum Project

## 1. Program Overview

| Subject | Reading & Writing |
|---|---|
| Age Band / Stage | 7–8 (Grade 2) |
| Duration | Full school year — approximately 36 weeks across 4 units |
| Learning Approach | Shifts from learning to read toward reading to learn — fluency, structured writing, grammar, and inference all get more formal |
| Standards Alignment | US: Common Core RF.2 / RL.2 / W.2  ·  Canada (ON): Grade 2 Language  ·  Global: Cambridge Primary English Stage 2–3 |
| Assessment Approach | Worksheet → quiz → unit test per unit, plus one cumulative exam guide for parents/teachers |


## 2. Scope & Sequence

Four units cover the full Grade 2 Reading & Writing year.

### Unit 1: Spelling Patterns & Fluency (Weeks 1–9)

- Read multisyllabic words using common spelling patterns (vowel teams, silent e)
- Read grade-level text with increasing fluency and expression
- Spell words using common patterns learned
- Use context clues to figure out unfamiliar words
Key vocabulary: syllable, vowel team, silent e, fluency, context clue

### Unit 2: Paragraph Writing (Weeks 10–18)

- Write a paragraph with a topic sentence, supporting details, and a closing sentence
- Organize ideas in a logical order
- Use transition words (first, next, then, finally)
- Revise writing for clarity with guidance
Key vocabulary: paragraph, topic sentence, detail, transition word

### Unit 3: Grammar Basics (Weeks 19–27)

- Identify and use nouns, verbs, and adjectives correctly
- Use correct subject-verb agreement
- Use commas in a list and in dates
- Identify and correct common grammar errors
Key vocabulary: noun, verb, adjective, subject-verb agreement, comma

### Unit 4: Reading Between the Lines (Weeks 28–36)

- Make inferences using text clues and background knowledge
- Identify cause and effect in a text
- Compare and contrast two texts or characters
- Support answers with evidence from the text
Key vocabulary: infer, cause, effect, compare, contrast, evidence

## 3. Unit 1: Spelling Patterns & Fluency


### Lesson Plan — “Cracking the Spelling Code”


**Objective**
Students will read and spell multisyllabic words using common patterns like vowel teams and silent e.

**Materials**
Word sort cards (vowel team words, silent e words), whiteboard, a short fluency passage.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Read a silent-e word pair aloud (hop/hope); ask what changed.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce silent e (changes a short vowel to long: hop → hope) and vowel teams (ai, ee, oa) with example words. Practice reading a few words together.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children sort word cards into ‘silent e’ and ‘vowel team’ categories.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 1 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children read a short fluency passage aloud, practicing smooth phrasing.

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on silent e only first, with very familiar word pairs. Stretch: introduce more complex vowel teams (ough, igh) for extra challenge.

## 4. Unit 2: Paragraph Writing


### Lesson Plan — “Building a Paragraph”


**Objective**
Students will write a paragraph with a topic sentence, supporting details, and a closing sentence.

**Materials**
Paragraph frame handout, whiteboard, picture writing prompts.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Show a jumbled paragraph (sentences out of order); discuss what order makes sense.

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Model writing a paragraph about a topic (e.g., ‘My Favorite Animal’) with a clear topic sentence, 2–3 detail sentences, and a closing sentence.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
As a class, build a paragraph together on a new topic, identifying each part.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 2 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children read their topic sentence aloud to a partner.

**Differentiation**
Support: provide a paragraph frame with sentence starters. Stretch: ask children to add a transition word to each detail sentence.

### Practice Worksheet

9. True or False: A strong paragraph usually has more than one sentence. ______

## 5. Unit 3: Grammar Basics


### Lesson Plan — “Nouns, Verbs, and Adjectives”


**Objective**
Students will identify and use nouns, verbs, and adjectives correctly in sentences.

**Materials**
Word sort cards (nouns, verbs, adjectives), sentence-building cards, whiteboard.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Ask: “What is a person, place, or thing called? What is an action word called?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Introduce nouns (person/place/thing), verbs (action), and adjectives (describing words) with examples. Build a sentence together, labeling each part.

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
In pairs, children sort word cards into noun, verb, and adjective categories.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 3 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Children build one sentence with at least one of each part of speech.

**Differentiation**
Support: focus on nouns and verbs only first. Stretch: introduce subject-verb agreement (he runs vs. they run) and comma usage in a list.

## 6. Unit 4: Reading Between the Lines


### Lesson Plan — “What the Text Doesn't Say”


**Objective**
Students will make inferences using text clues and identify cause and effect in a text.

**Materials**
A short story with inferable details, cause and effect picture cards.

**Warm-Up (5 minutes)**
Show a picture of someone holding an umbrella and wearing a raincoat; ask “What can you guess about the weather?”

**Main Activity (15 minutes)**
Read a short passage aloud. Model making an inference: ‘The text says Sam's stomach growled. What can we guess?’

**Guided Practice (10 minutes)**
As a class, identify 2–3 cause-and-effect pairs from a story or real-life scenario.

**Independent Practice (10 minutes)**
Children complete the Unit 4 practice worksheet.

**Wrap-Up / Check for Understanding (5 minutes)**
Ask children to share one inference they made while reading this week.

**Differentiation**
Support: use very obvious inference clues with picture support. Stretch: ask children to support their inference with a specific quote or detail from the text.

### Practice Worksheet

## 7. Cumulative Exam & Assessment Guide (For Parents & Teachers)

This guide covers the full Grade 2 Reading & Writing year (all 4 units).

**End-of-Year Skills Checklist**
- Reads multisyllabic words with vowel teams and silent e, with growing fluency
- Writes a structured paragraph with a topic sentence, details, and a closing sentence
- Uses nouns, verbs, and adjectives correctly, with subject-verb agreement and commas
- Makes inferences, identifies cause and effect, and compares and contrasts

**Question Types to Expect**
Word-pattern reading and spelling, paragraph construction, grammar identification and correction, and inference or cause-and-effect questions.

**Tips for Supporting Your Child**
- Read slightly more challenging text together and discuss what's implied but not stated
- Have your child narrate their day in paragraph form
- Play ‘what part of speech is this word’ games
- Point out cause-and-effect in everyday situations

**Suggested Pacing (Full Year)**
Each unit follows: Worksheet → 2–3 days of practice → Quiz → about 1 week more practice → Unit Test → move to the next unit, across roughly 36 weeks.
