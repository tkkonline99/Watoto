Privacy Policy — Watoto

Last updated: 2026-07-18

1. Who we are and what Watoto is

Watoto is a multilingual learning app for children. A parent or guardian creates and controls the account. Children do not create credentialed accounts and do not enter email addresses or phone numbers.

We design Watoto for children: no third-party ads, no sale of personal data, no open chat between children, and no external links on child-facing screens.

2. Data we collect

On the device (offline-first)

  • Child nickname (not legal name), avatar choice, approximate age band (birth year only)
  • Learning progress (lessons, quizzes, stories), XP, stars, coins, streaks, badges
  • Parent PIN or local account email/password (hashed on device)
  • Parental consent choices and accessibility preferences
  • Optional analytics flag (off by default)

Optional cloud (when Firebase is enabled by the family)

  • Parent account email and Firebase authentication identifiers
  • Consent ledger entries (policy version, method, time)
  • Synced progress under accounts/{parentUid}/profiles/{profileId}/progress/
  • Subscription entitlement fields written only by our servers (not by the app client)

We do not require a child’s real name, school ID, precise location, contacts, photos from the camera roll, or microphone recordings for core play (on-device text-to-speech may read captions without storing audio).

3. How we use data

  • Provide and improve learning activities and progress
  • Enforce parent controls (PIN, screen time, consent)
  • Security, abuse prevention, and crash diagnostics (configured without advertising IDs)
  • Comply with law and respond to parental requests

We do not use children’s data for behavioral advertising or to profile children for marketing.

4. Legal bases (where GDPR/UK GDPR applies)

  • Contract — operating features the parent requested
  • Consent — verifiable parental consent for a child’s personal data (Art. 8) and optional analytics
  • Legitimate interests — security and integrity of the service, balanced for children

Digital consent age follows applicable law (US COPPA under 13; EU/UK as set by member state, typically 13–16).

5. Parental consent (COPPA / children)

Before collecting personal information from a child under the applicable age, Watoto presents a parental gate and records consent (policy version, method, timestamp). Parents may review, export, refuse further collection, or delete data from the Parent Zone or by emailing privacy@watoto.app.

6. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Cloud infrastructure (when cloud is enabled) — Firebase / Google Cloud under our configuration
  • App stores — for distribution and (if you enable purchases later) billing
  • Law enforcement when required by valid legal process

We do not sell personal information.

7. International transfers

Default mode keeps progress on device. When cloud sync is enabled, data may be processed in regions selected for the Firebase project. Transfers from the EEA/UK use SCCs or other approved mechanisms where required.

8. Retention

Data Retention
On-device progress Until the parent deletes the profile or uninstalls / clears app data
Cloud account & progress While the account is active; purged within 30 days of verified deletion request (after any legal hold)
Consent evidence Retained as long as needed to demonstrate compliance, then deleted
Crash logs (if enabled) Up to 90 days

9. Security

We use industry-standard protections (HTTPS in transit for cloud, access rules so parents only see their own data, server-only entitlement fields). No method is 100% secure; parents should protect device unlock and the parent PIN.

10. Your rights

Parents may access, correct, export, or delete child data; withdraw consent; and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority where applicable. Use Parent Zone or email privacy@watoto.app. We aim to respond within 30 days.

11. Children

Watoto is directed to children with parental control. We do not knowingly allow a child to create a parent account.

12. Changes

We will update this policy and the in-app version when material practices change. Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
