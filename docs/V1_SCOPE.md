# v1 scope — which stages ship

**Decision: v1 is ages 0–6, stages 1–6. Stages 7–10 stay in the repo and out of
the build.**

Enforced by `maxPublishedStage` in
[`apps/mobile/lib/core/config/publish_config.dart`](../apps/mobile/lib/core/config/publish_config.dart),
guarded by [`apps/mobile/test/published_stages_test.dart`](../apps/mobile/test/published_stages_test.dart).

## Why

The library is wide and, above stage 6, shallow. Depth is measured per design
tier by `apps/mobile/test/lesson_depth_test.dart` — lessons 4/6/8/8 steps,
quizzes 2/3/4/4 questions, stories 4/5/6/6 pages, by tier.

| Stage | Ages | Tier | Lessons under depth | Quizzes under | Stories under | Playing time |
|------:|------|------|--------------------:|--------------:|--------------:|-------------:|
| 1 | 0–1 | parent-led | 0/30 | 10/20 | 10/10 | ~61 min |
| 2 | 1–2 | parent-led | 0/36 | 16/24 | 12/12 | ~74 min |
| 3 | 2–3 | parent-led | 0/36 | 15/24 | 12/12 | ~73 min |
| 4 | 3–4 | encourage-effort | 0/36 | 24/24 | 12/12 | ~95 min |
| 5 | 4–5 | encourage-effort | 0/30 | 20/20 | 10/10 | ~79 min |
| 6 | 5–6 | gamify-gently | **0/30** | **0/20** | **0/10** | **~127 min** |
| 7 | 6–7 | gamify-gently | 26/36 | 18/18 | 6/6 | ~61 min |
| 8 | 7–8 | grant-autonomy | 36/36 | 18/18 | 6/6 | ~37 min |
| 9 | 8–9 | grant-autonomy | 36/36 | 18/18 | 6/6 | ~36 min |
| 10 | 9–10 | grant-autonomy | 36/36 | 18/18 | 6/6 | ~52 min |

Playing time is beats × 20s, where a beat is one lesson step, quiz question or
story page. It is a rough figure and the comparison is what matters, not the
absolute.

A child only ever plays their own stage. That is the number that decides this:

- **Stage 6 is complete** on all three item types — the only stage that is.
- **Stages 1–5 hold their lesson depth**, which is the primary activity, and
  every item the build plan asks for exists. Their quizzes and stories are
  still thin; that is the next tranche of work, not a reason to withhold them.
- **Stages 8–10 have every one of their 36 lessons below depth** — two steps
  for an eight-year-old — and 36–52 minutes of content each. That is one
  sitting.
- **Stage 7 is worse than either**: 10 lessons were deepened as a pilot and 26
  were not, so a child gets a real lesson and then five hollow ones.

Shipping stages 7–10 would put "ages 0–10" on a store listing and hand the
7-to-10-year-old band about 40 minutes of two-step lessons. That is the parent
most likely to check what they bought. Ages 0–6 is a smaller claim and a true
one.

## What this does not do

It does not delete anything. `curriculumStages` still holds all ten stages
because the parent's curriculum guide is reference data, and the content
library still ships every item — the flag governs what a child is *offered*,
not what exists. `content/deepen_stage7.py` and the stage 7–10 items stay
exactly where they are.

## Reversing it

One flag, in this order of preference:

1. **Author the stage, then raise the default.** Follow
   `content/author_stage6.py`; the per-subject worklist is
   [CONTENT_WORKLIST.md](CONTENT_WORKLIST.md), regenerated by
   `node content/worklist.js`. Then set `defaultValue` to 7 and lower the three
   baselines in `lesson_depth_test.dart`.
2. **See the whole library in a dev build** without shipping it:
   `flutter run --dart-define=WATOTO_MAX_STAGE=10`.

The test suite passes at either setting — the guards are written against
`maxPublishedStage`, not against a literal 6, so raising it is a one-line
change and not a test rewrite.

## Consequences to remember at submission

- Store listing, age rating and screenshots must say **0–6**, not 0–10.
  [STORE_LISTING.md](STORE_LISTING.md) still says 0–10 and needs editing before
  submission.
- `effectiveFreeStageMax` now follows `maxPublishedStage`, so the free-only
  launch unlocks exactly what exists.
- A device that ran an earlier build with a higher cap can hold a
  `preferredStage` above it. `stageForChild` and the curriculum screen both
  clamp, so that resolves to the top published stage rather than an empty one.
