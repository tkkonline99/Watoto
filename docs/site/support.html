Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I access the Parent Zone?

Tap the Parent Zone icon from the profile selector or home navigation bar, solve the adult arithmetic challenge, and enter the 4-digit Parent PIN you created during family onboarding.

2. What if I forgot my Parent PIN?

If you signed in with a parent email and password on the device, you can reset your PIN after re-authenticating. If you use Watoto in offline-only mode and cannot recall your PIN, clearing the app’s storage in your device settings will reset the app (note: unsaved local progress will be erased unless backed up first).

3. How do I back up or transfer my child’s progress to a new device?

Open Parent Zone → Backup & Export to export your family’s progress as a JSON backup file, then import that file on your new device.

4. Read-aloud narration (Text-to-Speech) is silent on my device

Watoto uses your device’s built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine so narration works offline without sending audio to external servers. To enable voices for English, Kiswahili, or French:

  • iOS / iPadOS: Open Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices and download a voice for English, Swahili, or French.
  • Android: Open Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output → Preferred engine (Speech Recognition & Synthesis from Google) and install voice data for your preferred language.

5. How do I delete my child’s profile or all family data?

You can delete an individual child profile or wipe all local device data immediately at any time from Parent Zone → Data & Privacy. If you have enabled optional cloud sync and wish to delete cloud records without signing into the app, email privacy@watoto.app from your registered parent email address and we will purge your account within 30 days.
